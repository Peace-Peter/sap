Welcome to my personal SAP blog. I am a dedicated SAP Financial Accounting & Controlling (FICO) Consultant. I share practical, step-by-step guides, configuration walkthroughs, and accounting posting logic to help SAP professionals, consultants, and end-users master enterprise financial processes.

My core expertise spans across General Ledger (FI-GL), Accounts Payable (FI-AP), Accounts Receivable (FI-AR), Asset Accounting (FI-AA), and Bank Accounting (FI-BL), as well as end-to-end integration cycles like Procure-to-Pay (P2P) and Order-to-Cash (O2C).
